# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students, educators, and presentation audiences exploring an interactive story about generative AI in higher education.

## Product Purpose

Use the HEPI / Kortext Student Generative AI Survey 2026 to show how the same tool can fit into different student experiences. The screen-world narrative connects survey evidence to Arman's and Ben's study situations.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-led, humane, reflective. Preserve the established fictional software interfaces and contrasting student workspaces.

## Anti-references

Not a dashboard or a marketing landing page. Do not frame either student as morally superior, copy real app branding exactly, or add decoration that competes with the data.

## Design Principles

- Make the evidence legible before adding atmosphere.
- Show perspective through student actions and consistent interface identities.
- Keep real survey quotations distinct from fictional conversations.
- Use motion to clarify narrative order, not to obstruct navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, direct chart labels, and reduced-motion readability. Color must not be the only way to distinguish data or meaning.
